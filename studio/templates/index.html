              How the studio works

              Turn a script into a recording in your own voice, in three steps. Set up your voice once under Voice and words; after that, most recordings take a few minutes of your attention.

              1. 1

                Script

                Paste or import your text. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.

                Select Preview spoken to read exactly what the voice will say. Dates, prices, times and abbreviations are written out for you, and highlighted. If a name or brand will come out wrong, select it and type how to say it.

              2. 2

                Settings

                Choose the voice engine: Chatterbox Turbo is fast; Maximum resemblance sounds most like you but takes longer. Once you have trained your voice, Trained voice speaks with it, with or without your reference clip.

                Optionally add a delivery style, such as "warm, conversational narrator", for the whole script or per paragraph. Then select Generate voice-over.

              3. 3

                Review and export

                Each section is checked automatically. A green dot passed; an amber dot needs a listen.

                Select a section to fix its words or try another take, then download the WAV or MP3.

              Set up your voice once

              • Record 10–30 seconds of yourself in a quiet room, in the style you want to sound like, and save it as a WAV.
              • Add it under Voice and words, then type exactly what you said in Reference transcript. Maximum resemblance needs it.
              • Keep several references, such as conversational and energetic, and switch between them.

              Pronunciations

              • Spell how a word sounds as one lowercase word, like noovexuh for Nuvexa.
              • Saved words apply to every recording made afterwards.
              • Avoid hyphens and capitals inside the spelling; the studio removes them because voices read them as pauses or letters.

              Reviewing sections

              • Needs review shows only flagged sections, with what the transcription heard.
              • Regenerate section makes a new take. Every take is kept; choose one under Takes.
              • More options holds the pause after a section and advanced settings.

              Training your voice

              • Under Voice training, read short prompts aloud; each take is checked for the right words, clipping and noise.
              • Aim for 30 minutes of kept recordings, in several short sessions. Add your own scripts for the best results.
              • Export training set creates the folder used to train a version of the model on your voice.

              Delivering the file

              • Choose Delivery loudness to match where it will play: −16 LUFS for web and podcasts, −14 for online video, −23 or −24 for broadcast.
              • Changing it rebuilds the files without generating speech again.
              • Listen to the whole recording before you send it. Checks help, but your ear decides.
